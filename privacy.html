Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Casa Arrayyan ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and shared when you play our Android game Penjelajah BBM.

Information We Collect

The game does not require you to create an account or provide personal information directly. However, third-party advertising and mediation services used by the game may automatically collect or process certain information, which may include:

  • Advertising or device identifiers
  • Device and technical information
  • IP address and network information
  • Approximate location information where applicable
  • Crash and diagnostic information
  • App performance information
  • Advertising and app interaction information, such as ad impressions and clicks

Penjelajah BBM does not request precise location permission. The information processed may vary depending on the advertising services, advertising partners, device, region, applicable laws, and privacy settings.

Advertising and Mediation

The game uses Appodeal as its advertising mediation platform. Appodeal helps select and deliver advertisements from enabled advertising demand partners.

The current advertising configuration may include:

  • Appodeal — advertising mediation and related SDK services.
  • AppLovin — an advertising demand partner.
  • Pangle through BidMachine — an advertising demand partner integrated through BidMachine.

These services may process device, advertising, network, performance, diagnostic, and advertising interaction information to provide, measure, secure, and improve advertising services.

Depending on applicable laws, regional requirements, user settings, age-related requirements, and consent, advertisements may be personalized or non-personalized.

How Information Is Used

Information processed by the advertising and mediation services may be used for purposes including:

  • Delivering advertisements
  • Measuring advertising performance and impressions
  • Preventing fraud, abuse, and invalid advertising activity
  • Analyzing app performance and stability
  • Improving advertising and app services
  • Complying with applicable legal and regulatory requirements

Children's Privacy

Penjelajah BBM is available to a broad audience, including users who may be under 13. The game does not require children to provide personal information directly through the game.

Advertising and mediation services may apply child-directed, age-related, consent, and non-personalized advertising restrictions where required by applicable law or platform policy. The processing of advertising information may therefore differ depending on the user's age, region, consent status, and applicable privacy settings.

Data Security

I do not directly sell users' personal information. Third-party services used by the game may process information in accordance with their own privacy policies and terms.

Reasonable measures are taken to protect information handled by the game. Data transmitted by the game and its enabled third-party services may be protected using encryption in transit.

Data Retention and Deletion

Penjelajah BBM does not maintain a user account or user profile that can be managed directly inside the game. The game therefore does not provide an in-app account deletion function.

Information processed by third-party advertising and mediation providers is handled according to their respective privacy policies and retention practices. Where applicable, users may use the privacy controls or request mechanisms provided by those services.

Depending on your device, region, age, consent status, and applicable law, you may have choices regarding personalized advertising and the processing of advertising identifiers. You may also use available device-level advertising and privacy controls.
